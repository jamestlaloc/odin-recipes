Tomato Soup

Description

There is

NOTHING
better on a cold day than a scrumptious, and hot tomato soup with a little kick to it. There's no better place to find the best recipe for one, than here! Once you finish following this recipe, you'll never take tomatoes for granted ever again. Don't forget to grab some bread and chili flakes to pair with your soup!

If this soup converts you into a tomato lover, share the recipe with a friend!

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes
  • Veggie Stock
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Roasted Red Peppers
  • Jalapenos

Steps

  1. Sautee' onions and garlic until translucent.
  2. Add peeled tomatoes, veggie stock, and roasted red peppers.
  3. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 30min or until desired consistency.
