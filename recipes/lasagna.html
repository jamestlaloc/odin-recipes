Description

Oh boy! You're going to love this lasagna recipe. Some would say, it's the best recipe! Just like grandma used to make - and now, you'll be able to make it yourself and brag to your friends about it!

If you're happy with how this recipe turned out for you, just like with ANY good dish, share it!

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Sauce
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Layer the pasta, sauce, and cheese, in that order, many times until desired height.
  2. Place it in the oven at 375 degrees for 1 hour
  3. Takeout the lasagna (careful, it's hot!), slice it howeever you want, and enjoy!
